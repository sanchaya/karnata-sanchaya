# Data we still need

The atlas is only as good as its sources. This is the list of datasets and sources we are looking for, in plain language, so that anyone
(a department, a researcher, a museum, a birder, a teacher) can point us to them. It is mirrored on the portal under
**Project & contribute > Data wanted** (`src/data/data-wanted.js`, shown by `src/DataWanted.jsx`), where each item has an
**"I know a source"** button that opens the contribution form. Keep this file and that list in step; a test checks it.

## What we can use

We can use data that is **in the public domain, openly licensed (CC0, CC BY, ODbL, an Indian government open-data licence such as
NDSAP/GODL-India), or whose owner agrees in writing to non-commercial redistribution with attribution.** This project is
**non-commercial** (see [`LICENSING.md`](../LICENSING.md)). Please do not send anything you do not have the right to share, and tell us
the licence and where it came from. A link and a licence are more useful than a file.

## The list

Each item: why we need it, what exactly would help, who might hold it. Priority: **high** = blocks a page that is already live.

### Geography and environment
- **forest-boundaries** *(high)*: **Statutory reserved-forest, wildlife-sanctuary and national-park boundaries.** The geography
  page shows approximate points and links to Bhuvan. Needed: boundary polygons with notification numbers and dates, under an open
  licence or written permission. Held by: Karnataka Forest Department, NRSC Bhuvan, Wildlife Institute of India.
- **rainfall**: **Rainfall normals** by taluk or district, or a gridded map. Replaces the broad bands on the region cards. Held by:
  India Meteorological Department, Karnataka State Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre (KSNDMC).
- **soil**: **Soil map** of Karnataka (soil types as polygons). Held by: ICAR-NBSS&LUP, Karnataka State Remote Sensing Applications
  Centre, Department of Agriculture.
- **geology**: **Geological map** (lithology polygons, schist belts, mines). Held by: Geological Survey of India, Department of Mines
  and Geology.
- **catchments**: **River basin and sub-basin polygons**, and survey-grade river courses. We draw simplified OpenStreetMap lines now.
  Held by: India-WRIS, Survey of India, Water Resources Department, HydroSHEDS.
- **caves-mangroves**: **Natural cave inventory** (limestone and laterite caves) and **mangrove extents**. Held by: Forest Department,
  Space Applications Centre, researchers.
- **verified-places**: **Verified coordinates and heights** for the approximate points: dams, peaks, waterfalls, hill stations, coastal
  sites. Held by: Survey of India toposheets, KSNDMC, India-WRIS, a gazetteer.
- **state-boundaries**: **State boundaries as on the official map of India**, at state level (Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh in full), for the
  dynasty and language maps. Held by: Survey of India.

### Species and nature
- **kannada-names**: **Vetted Kannada names of birds, animals, reptiles, amphibians, insects and plants.** Only about a quarter of
  our species have any suggestion and none is reviewed. Needed: field guides, dictionaries, biodiversity registers or lists
  that may be cited, with page locators. Held by: Karnataka Biodiversity Board, universities, naturalist groups, authors.
- **species-checklists**: **Complete, authoritative Karnataka checklists** (not only the most-recorded species): birds, mammals,
  reptiles, amphibians, butterflies, dragonflies, trees and other plants, under an open licence. Held by: Karnataka Bird Records
  Committee, BNHS, ATREE, IISc, Flora of Karnataka authors.
- **bird-tracking**: **Tracked migration data** (ringing recoveries, satellite telemetry) to replace our sighting-based origins.
  Held by: BNHS, Wildlife Institute of India, Movebank studies with open licences.
- **bird-calls**: **Openly licensed bird-call recordings** for Karnataka species, to play in the page rather than link out. Held by:
  Xeno-canto contributors (CC licences), research groups.

### History, heritage and language
- **inscription-texts**: **Open editions or transcriptions of Karnataka inscriptions** (Epigraphia Carnatica, Karnataka Inscriptions,
  South Indian Inscriptions, Annual Reports on Indian Epigraphy) with rights cleared. We hold only pointers today. Held by:
  Archaeological Survey of India, Department of Archaeology, Museums and Heritage, universities, libraries.
- **heritage-photos**: **Openly licensed photographs** of monuments, inscriptions, coins, manuscripts and artefacts, with author and
  licence. Held by: photographers, museums, Wikimedia Commons contributors.
- **periodicals**: **Digitised Kannada periodicals and newspapers** that are out of copyright or shared with permission. Held by:
  libraries, archives, publishers.
- **gazetteers**: **District gazetteers and census handbooks** (digitised, with licence). Held by: Karnataka Gazetteer Department,
  Directorate of Census Operations, state archives.
- **district-data**: **Taluk and village boundaries, place-name histories and heritage inventories** under an open licence. Held by:
  Survey Settlement and Land Records Department, Local Government Directory, district administrations.
- **museum-collections**: **Museum and temple collection catalogues** (object lists, dates, provenance) that may be cited and linked.
  Held by: Government and private museums, temple trusts, ASI.

## How to send us a source

1. On the portal: **Project & contribute > Data wanted**, open the item and choose **I know a source** (a free account is needed; it
   opens the contribution form for a new source, which a reviewer checks).
2. Or write to **info@sanchifoundation.org** with the item name, a link, the licence or permission, and who holds it.
3. If you are the holder, tell us under what terms we may use it. We will credit you.

## How an item is closed

A source is accepted when a reviewer has confirmed (a) it is the right data, (b) the licence or permission allows our non-commercial
use with attribution, and (c) it is recorded in [`LICENSING.md`](../LICENSING.md) §3 with where it is used. Then the item moves out of
this list.
