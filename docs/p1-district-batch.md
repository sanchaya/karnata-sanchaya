The eight audits contain 16 candidate sites. Every candidate now has a
site/locality map point and none uses a district-centre fallback. Six points
remain approximate, locality, settlement or precinct-level and must not be
presented as surveyed monument coordinates. The other ten are recorded as
`point` or `site`, but still require authority confirmation.

No candidate currently meets the full publication gate because none has an
authority-confirmed, dated present-condition packet together with every other
required evidence check. The data merger now prevents a legacy `verified`
label from surviving when that condition evidence is absent. Discovery maps
and tourism pages remain leads, not final evidence.
