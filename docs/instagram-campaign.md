# Instagram campaign: support and fund the atlas

Goal: turn readers and players into donors and contributors, and be honest about what the project is. Nothing here has been posted. The Kannada is a draft and
**needs a native speaker's proofreading before posting**. Creatives: `node scripts/share-cards/campaign.mjs output/campaign` (needs Playwright; set
`PLAYWRIGHT=<path to playwright-core index.mjs>` if it is not installed in the project). It writes 1080×1350 PNGs, `NN-name.kn.png` and `NN-name.en.png`. The numbers on
the images are read from `public/data/open/atlas-summary.json` at render time, so re-run it after `npm run build` and before posting.

## Idea and tone

**"Our history, in our language"** (ನಮ್ಮ ಇತಿಹಾಸ, ನಮ್ಮ ಭಾಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ). The honest hook is that a lot is built and much is still unchecked: 48,429 records, 2,078 sources, 8,259 relationships, and
38,571 leads waiting for a person to review them. People fund work they can see is real and unfinished. Tone: plain, warm, Kannada first, no guilt, no hard sell.
Never promise outcomes, never show a fundraising target the owners have not set, and never invent testimonials or beneficiary counts.

Audiences: Kannada readers and diaspora (the emotional case), teachers and parents (games, worksheet), history and wildlife enthusiasts (sources, bird names),
institutions and CSR contacts (grants, sponsor-a-topic: say "coming soon" only as the site does).

## Reel / story ideas (no new creative needed)

- **30-second tour:** screen-record the atlas: open a dynasty, tap an inscription, show its source and "needs review" tag, end on the Support page. Caption as 06.
- **Game of the day story:** screenshot of that day's game with the link sticker.
- **Name this bird:** a species photo from the atlas (Wikimedia Commons, credit the photographer and licence in the caption) with a poll sticker.
- **Counter story:** the "records still waiting for review" number, updated weekly.

## Before posting (owner decisions)

1. **Handles and bio link.** Set the real Instagram handle in `VITE_SOCIAL_INSTAGRAM_URL`; never post a guessed handle. Bio link: `karnata.sanchaya.net/#donate`.
2. **Fund-use list (post 06).** The three items (checking records, digitising books and inscriptions, keeping the site online) describe the project's work; confirm with Sanchi Foundation that they match how donations are used before posting. Do not add rupee amounts or a goal unless the Foundation sets one.
3. **80G (post 08).** The wording comes from the site's own Support page. Confirm it is current with the Foundation.
4. **Kannada proofreading** of every caption and image text.
5. **Privacy.** The images show only the public UPI QR and the foundation email. Do not add bank account numbers, PAN or donor names; show a donor or contributor only with their agreement.
6. **Images and sources.** The campaign uses no third-party photos. For reels and stories, credit the photographer and licence for any bird photo, and do not show names from sources on hold (Hakkipukka).
7. **Robots.** `robots.txt` stays as is; Instagram posts do not change it.

## Measuring

Track per post: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link-in-bio taps. On the site, donations arrive by UPI or bank transfer and cannot be attributed to a post unless donors say
how they found us. Add "How did you hear about us?" to the receipt email reply, and use `?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=support` on bio links (check once that the page still opens on the Support section).
