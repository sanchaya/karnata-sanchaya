# Atlas v0.21 — political-relations review queue

The first bilateral pass is a research queue, not a completed historical verdict. Every record in `atlasData.politicalRelations` is intentionally `needs-review` and carries the same six evidence gates:

1. party identity and the relationship type;
2. date and sequence of the relationship;
3. route and battle locations;
4. outcome and degree of control;
5. rulers, commanders and other people;
6. treaty, inscription, chronicle or archival-document witness.

## Reviewer procedure

For each record, reviewers should update only the relevant checklist item after attaching a source locator. A relation may move from `unresolved` to `located` when a dependable witness is found, but it remains `needs-review` until an independent reviewer confirms the interpretation. A route or battle marker must not be upgraded to an exact coordinate from a schematic map alone.

The admin portal exposes these records under **Bilateral political relations**. The public `#relations` page shows them with the pending style, so the map remains useful while uncertainty stays visible. Promotion requires a documented decision on all six gates and does not automatically change the related event’s review status.
